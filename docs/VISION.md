# Gnome Life — the vision in one page (decided 2026-10-25)

Everything here was chosen by Bryce in the "Pin down the game's vision" thread (question set and answers: `VISION-QUESTIONS.md`). Earlier decisions (art style, isometric view, gentle timers, southern seasons, owned art only) still stand; see `PLAN.md`. The staff mystery is Bryce's to write and is not described here.

## The story (Bryce, 2026-10-25)
A cosy game with no real stakes. The main storyline is **a sweet story of Wendel and Hazel's love over their marriage**, with small tribulations brought by visitors and neighbours (a forgotten anniversary, too long in the workshop, a worry about winter), always mended warmly. Each neighbour's little story shows one such moment. The **magic staff story is 100% optional**, late game, started by an object found in mysterious circumstances; it may never happen, and Bryce writes it.

## The game in a paragraph
You are an unseen helper spirit. A young newlywed gnome couple, **Wendel** (the dreamer: tinkers, invents, gazes at stars, loses his pipe) and **Hazel** (the practical one: organises, gardens, keeps them fed, dry wit), arrive in a logged, human-scarred clearing. Over three to six months of real time you help them heal the land tile by tile, turn a root-shelter into a snug home, and welcome wandering gnomes who stay when you build them a house. The result is a cosy village tucked into nature, never a city. A festival marks the soft ending, and then the world keeps living: seasons turn, visitors come, the couple slowly grows old.

## The world
- **Size:** about 25 tiles (5x5). One tile is the current diamond, about 3.7 gnomes across.
- **Spread:** ripples out from the home tile. Each healed tile reveals its misty neighbours, which you coax green.
- **Barren look:** a mix of scars: bare plain and stumps (have), plus a dry creek bed, an old fence or rubbish, and one more variant.
- **Finished look:** a gnome village embedded in nature. More than half of every tile stays wild (ferns, toadstools, roots, water). Buildings sit under or inside trees, roots and stumps, never in rows. A stream and a path wind through; a bridge crosses the stream.
- **Seasons:** colour tints and overlays done in code (autumn tones, winter mist, spring blossom) plus seasonal visitors and events. No seasonal tile sets.

## The couple
- Names **Wendel** and **Hazel**. Personalities as above; gentle comedy between them, both kind.
- They **age slowly** over many months: young now, middle-aged around the village stage, old in the long tail. Three character art sets over time.
- **Children:** maybe later; leave room in story and save, build nothing yet.
- **Daily rhythm on the real clock:** wake, breakfast by the fire, work, an evening sit (Wendel's pipe, Hazel's knitting), sleep. Visit at night and you find them asleep or by the fire.

## A visit
- **Main loop:** 2-5 minutes, 2-3 times a day (morning, after work, bedtime). Jobs take roughly 3-8 hours.
- **Quick check-ins** when bored must always find something small: a dew drop to collect, a chat line, a pot to move, a 10-20 minute job.
- **Ending a visit:** a clear gentle "all done for now" moment (a line from Hazel or Wendel, the screen settles), while idle fiddling (decorating, chatting, collecting) stays open.
- **Words:** warm, a little old-fashioned, lightly funny storybook narrator. A line or two in normal play; milestones open a small illustrated story page.
- **No notifications, no sound** for now.

## Home
- **Three stages:** (1) shelter under a root with campfire (have); (2) snug house in a stump or under an oak; (3) house plus garden, well and workshop.
- **Interior:** tap the house to open a painted cut-away room, one picture per stage. The evening routine plays out there.
- **Decorating:** a modest set of loose items you place and drag around the home tile: bench, lantern, flower pots, washing line, fence bits.

## Village
- **Growth:** travelling gnomes visit; build them a home and they stay. You choose who stays.
- **Size:** 6-10 neighbours by the end. Cast (built 2026-10-25): Mabel the baker, Tobias the carpenter, the Fernsby family, Bert and Mags Pebbleworth (an old married couple with an orchard), Clem the beekeeper, Barnaby the miller, Nettle the weaver, Juniper the lamplighter. **No sage** (Bryce, 2026-10-25).
- **Depth:** each has a trade, a handful of sayings, and a little quest of their own. Roughly 8-10 mini-quests over the game.
- **Your role:** you mainly help Wendel and Hazel; neighbours occasionally ask small favours. Never a whole-village manager.
- **First buildings:** well, bakery oven, workshop, bridge. Later candidates: mill, orchard, beehives, pond, meeting hall, inn, market stalls.

## Creatures and spirits
- **Animals:** mostly Australian (fairy-wren, echidna, possum, blue-tongue, kookaburra, frog, and the rest of the current 20) plus a few honorary classics from the gnome books (hedgehog, owl, mouse).
- **Spirits:** a few, rare and special: a spirit of the land, will-o'-wisps at night, maybe one mischievous troll-ish nuisance.
- **Journal:** small painted portrait, one warm line of lore, date first seen.
- **Regulars:** leave out seed or berries and some creatures turn up daily and may nest nearby. No named pets.

## Magic
- **For healing land and gentle weather only:** coax sprouts, call rain, wake a tree, clear mist. Grows by real days visited (as built).
- **Light** (the one magical resource) is gathered at night from wisps and dew AND earned by good deeds, so daytime players are not punished.
- The staff mystery is separate and Bryce's.

## Pacing
Home tile green in 1-2 weeks; proper house around a month; village by about half a year; ageing over a year or more. Soft ending at the village festival; the world keeps living after.
