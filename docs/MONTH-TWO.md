## The rule for month two: one big thing a week, one small thing a day

The calendar sets the pace, not how fast the player taps. Busy and relaxed players then meet things on about the same days.

- **Big things** (about one a week): a traveller coming up the track; a neighbour's story page; the whole creek running; the festival idea; home stage three; the festival night.
- **Small things** (about one a day, already in the engine): an animal beat, a neighbour's wish, a land-waking card, a farm or patch job, a friend's ask at the dish.
- **Jobs list: no more than about 6 waiting rows.** A neighbour's farm opens one job at a time and only after their story; festival jobs open one every 4 or 5 days.
- **New land:** at most one new patch every 3 days. The near side of the creek (20 patches) opens before the festival. **The far bank** (the 5 patches over the bridge) opens with the bridge but heals mostly after the festival, one patch a fortnight: the long tail.
