# Gnome Life art days: the mix-and-match kit and every picture still to paint (written 2026-10-25)

This one list replaces the prompts in `ART-BATCH-3.md` to `ART-BATCH-8.md` (those files stay for their wiring notes). It was written after the blended ground went live (`GRAPHICS-RETHINK.md`, PR #24) and after Bryce said: *"I love little details and Easter eggs... I feel like you need a little collection of various things to mix and match."*

**74 pictures in 7 art days, in the order to paint.** A "day" is just a good stopping point. Bryce runs them in Gemini from the Mac folder `~/Downloads/Gnome Art Day/`: `3 - Steps.txt` has every step in plain words, `4 - Prompts to copy/Day N - .../` has one text file per prompt (open, Cmd+A, Cmd+C), reference pictures are in `1 - Reference pictures/`, and finished pictures go in `2 - Put finished pictures here/`. Cloud copies: `/mnt/project-files/gnome-art-day/kit/`.

## The idea

- **A kit, not one-off pictures.** The ground is now built by the game from small pieces, so the most useful art is a collection of small things in several versions: plain ground patches, and sheets of tufts, pebbles, flowers, leaves, reeds, stones and marks. The game mixes them, so no two corners of the garden look the same.
- **Before and after in one sheet.** Stumps, rocks, branches, brambles and logs are painted dead (as now) and healed (mossy, sprouting) in the same sheet, so the land can visibly heal piece by piece instead of a whole tile swapping.
- **Little surprises everywhere.** Two sheets of tiny hidden finds (a four-leaf clover, a cicada shell, a fairy ring, a bowerbird's blue treasures, a lost thimble, a knitted mitten, a wax-sealed letter...), a sheet of old human leftovers that tiny gnomes see as giant (a bottle cap as wide as a hat), and small touches in the existing pictures: a darned heart on a sock, a heart carved by the door, a pie on a neighbour's sill, the lopsided honey-seed cake at the feast, a stitched heart on one bunting flag.
- **The hidden snail.** The same little garden snail hides in both rooms and in every story painting, for sharp eyes to find. (The garden snail is also one of the visitors.) Easy to drop if Bryce would rather not.
- **Nothing here is story.** The finds are just finds; Bryce decides later if any of them mean something.

## Rules every sheet follows

- Swatches (day 1) are full squares with no background: even all over, no border, no light falling across them, seen from straight above. The cutting tool squashes them to the game's slanted view and makes them repeat without a seam.
- Every other piece is on flat magenta with clear gaps, no ground patch and no shadow. Sticker sheets are 3 rows of 4 (cut with `tools/cut_cc.py`, reading order). Flat things are painted as if seen from above at the game's slant.
- No pink or purple in kit pieces (the magenta key would eat them), so the meadow flowers are white, yellow, blue, cream and red.
- Glows are never painted (ghost fungus, fireflies, lamps); the game adds them, so night can light them up.
